Case study

6.6M reached in under 3 months

How directing 20+ influencers under one brand standard put Aerosoles' latest collection in front of millions of new people.

Client
Aerosoles, via Giant Propeller
Role
Social Media & Partnerships Manager
Year
2022-2024
The challenge

20+ creators, one brand standard.

  • Launch Aerosoles' latest collection and grow brand awareness on Instagram through an influencer-led campaign.
  • Brief and direct 20+ influencers at once, keeping every piece coherent and on-brand.
  • Manage reputation across all partner-produced content, in step with the paid media department, inside a tight window.
The result

A pipeline built for brand control.

  • Ran the full influencer pipeline: partner selection, creative briefs, and asset review and approval before anything went live.
  • Directed 20+ posts, reels and stories with one visual and messaging identity across a varied roster.
  • Monitored performance and brand safety in real time for the whole campaign.
  • The result: 6.6M accounts reached, with 91% of engagement coming from non-followers.
Campaign results overview
6.6M

Accounts reached, up +70.8% on the prior period.

33.2M

Total impressions, up +69.3%.

76.4K

Accounts engaged, up +47.4%.

91%

Of engagement came from non-followers: new audiences.
