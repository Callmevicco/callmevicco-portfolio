Case study

PDF into product: 60K from a little tweak

How changing one thing transformed a quarterly PDF into a lead gen engine.

Client
Nexton
Role
Organic content specialist
Year
2024-2026
The challenge

No one reads PDFs anymore.

  • A lengthy document with A LOT of data isn't the best way to attract or nurture leads.
  • It was key to find a way to make the data more accessible and shareable.
The result

Adapt it to something better.

  • A boring PDF that became a dynamic product that updates every day.
  • Lives behind a sign-up wall that collects leads.
  • Offers super valuable data on the hiring market in LATAM vs the US.
  • Then it became the center of a lifecycle marketing campaign.
  • The perfect recipe for a lead magnet.
Before - Static PDF
After - Dynamic Product
60K

In revenue for that quarter after signing a new client.

50+

Generated leads in two months.

3x

Traffic to the site increased compared to the past year.
