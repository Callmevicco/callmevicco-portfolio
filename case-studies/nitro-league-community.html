Case study

4K+ followers in under 60 days

How a skeptical gaming audience became a community, built from scratch with native content and a 7-person remote team.

Client
Nitro League, via G63 Media
Role
Social Media & Brand Manager
Year
2021-2022
The challenge

A skeptical crowd, a launch date.

  • Build an engaged X community from the ground up for Nitro League, a Web3 racing game, ahead of a major update.
  • Win over a gaming and crypto audience that is skeptical, community-driven and fluent in Discord and X culture.
  • Lead a 7-person remote team across content, design and video to ship a full launch campaign at pace.
The result

Speak native, then lead the launch.

  • Researched Discord and X to learn the community's language, memes and formats, then built a native content strategy.
  • Led the full launch campaign, managing copywriters, designers and video editors to keep quality and brand consistency.
  • Curated a Spotify playlist as a community launch asset that tied the brand to its culture.
  • The result: 4,000+ new followers in under 2 months and a 12x spike in organic mentions.
